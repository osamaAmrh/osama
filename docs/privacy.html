Privacy Policy – Glow Sort

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Glow Sort ("the app") is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information is handled when you play it.

Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress (current level, coins, themes and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this information.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks, rewarded ads watched)
  • Crash and diagnostic data related to ad delivery

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time from Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all game data stored on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising processing by Google described above, no data is shared with third parties.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: osamaamrh@gmail.com
