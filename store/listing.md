```
Glow Sort is a calm, colorful ball sort puzzle that is easy to learn and hard to put down.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a tube to pick up the top ball, then tap another tube to drop it.
• A ball can only go on top of a ball of the same color, or into an empty tube.
• Fill every tube with a single color to clear the level.

FEATURES
• 2000+ levels – every single one is verified solvable
• Difficulty that grows with you, from 2 colors up to 12
• Undo moves and restart any time
• Stuck? Get a hint or add an extra tube
• Beautiful glowing visuals and satisfying sounds
• Unlock 6 themes: Midnight, Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Candy and Galaxy
• Daily gift of free coins
• Color-blind friendly mode with symbols on every ball
• Fully playable offline – no Wi-Fi needed
• English and Arabic

Train your brain, relax after a long day, and enjoy the most soothing way to sort colors. Download Glow Sort for free now!
```
